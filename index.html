I am a PhD candidate at the Lab for AI in Medicine at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), supervised by Prof. Daniel Rueckert and Prof. Benedikt Wiestler, whose AI for Image-Guided Diagnosis and Therapy (AI-IDT) lab I am also part of.

My research is on deep learning for medical image analysis, mostly of the brain: adapting vision foundation models to multi-modal imaging, self-supervised representation learning, domain generalization under distribution shift, and generative models for image-to-image translation.

Before my PhD, I received my bachelor's degree in applied computer science from DHBW Mannheim and my master's degree in informatics from TUM. Along the way I worked on machine learning at Gleamer, BMW, ENERGY4U and Atos.
