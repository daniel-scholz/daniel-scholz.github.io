## About Me

Daniel Scholz is a PhD candidate at the Institute for AI and Informatics in Medicine at the
Technical University of Munich, advised by Prof. Daniel Rueckert and Prof. Benedikt Wiestler.
His research focuses on medical image analysis — primarily brain imaging — spanning
self-supervised representation learning, generative models, and robust segmentation under
distribution shift. His first-author work has appeared at MICCAI, MIDL, and in
*Radiology: Artificial Intelligence*.
